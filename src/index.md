## What the course is

The course trains wellness coaches. NASM defines wellness coaching as the active process of applying learned and developed strategies that lead to the mental, emotional and physical betterment of life. Underneath every strategy is one thing: **the choices a client makes**. The coach does not tell clients what to do. The coach helps them see what is influencing their health, choose their own next steps, and build habits they can eventually keep up **without the coach**.

Two words come up again and again in the course:

- **Balance**: stability between stressors and the ability to cope, across all areas of wellness.
- **Consistency**: small adjustments to behaviour that add up to significant long-term change.

## How to use this wiki

- **Learning the course in order?** Follow the sidebar from top to bottom. It roughly follows the lesson order, with related lessons pulled together.
- **Looking for a specific lesson?** The [Lesson Map](lesson-map.md) lists all 73 lessons and the page that covers each one.
- **Need a quick definition?** See the [Glossary](glossary.md) for acronyms (OPSISE, OARS, DARN, CAT, PERMA) and key terms.
- **Preparing for a session?** Every concept page ends with **Coaching in practice**, a set of example questions and phrases, and **Related** links.
- **Search:** press `S` or click the magnifier to search every page.

Quotes are short excerpts (under about 25 words) from the lesson transcripts. The rest is paraphrase. See [About the Source](about.md).
