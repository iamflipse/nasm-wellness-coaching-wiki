# Glossary

Definitions are paraphrased from the course. The lesson numbers point to where each term comes up; follow the link for the full context.

| Term | Definition | Lesson(s) / page |
|---|---|---|
| **Active listening** | Taking in fully what the client says, then asking permission before sharing your own knowledge | 49 · [Coach's Role](concepts/coach-role-relationship.md) |
| **Affirmation** | The "A" in OARS: a true, behaviour-based statement that helps clients notice their strengths and values | 10, 56 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **Allostatic load** | The cumulative wear and tear on the body from chronically activated stress responses | 32 · [Stress](concepts/stress-and-recovery.md) |
| **Ambivalence** | Having conflicting desires about change | 3, 10, 61 · [OPSISE](concepts/wellness-coaching-opsise.md) |
| **Amplified reflection** | Repeating a client's all-or-nothing statement back in exaggerated form so they can reconsider it | 73 · [Derailers](concepts/derailers.md) |
| **Attribution / attributional retraining** | What a person blames for blocking them (attribution), and retraining thoughts and perceptions to get past it with small, doable steps | 8 · [Locus of Control](concepts/locus-of-control-attribution.md) |
| **Autophagy** | Described in the course as the body's clean-up of metabolic waste: "carrying out the trash" at the cellular level | 38 · [Dietary Patterns](concepts/healthy-dietary-patterns.md) |
| **BDNF** | A factor raised by exercise that builds new connections in the brain | 15, 21 · [Movement Benefits](concepts/movement-benefits.md) |
| **Beginner's mind with an expert toolkit** | Bringing both competence and open curiosity; seeing each client as unique | 50, 63, 67 · [Coaching Mindset](concepts/coaching-mindset.md) |
| **Body–food choice congruence** | Awareness of how particular foods make your body feel (energy, fullness, nourishment) | 43 · [Eating & Weight](concepts/eating-emotions-weight.md) |
| **CAT** | Change-talk prompts: Commitment, Activation, Taking steps | 60 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **Change talk** | Client language that signals motivation to change | 60 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **Coaching container** | The safe, consistent space and structure in which coaching happens | 6, 68 · [Coach's Role](concepts/coach-role-relationship.md) |
| **Coaching communication culture** | The intentional way a coach relates to a client, combining the coach's style, training and the client's preferences | 62 · [Cultural Awareness](concepts/cultural-awareness-philosophy.md) |
| **Coaching from center, not as center** | Coaching from a grounded place as a guide, not as the director of the client's journey | 50, 67 · [Coaching Mindset](concepts/coaching-mindset.md) |
| **Coaching philosophy** | A written statement of the core values and approaches behind your way of coaching | 63, 64 · [Philosophy](concepts/cultural-awareness-philosophy.md) |
| **Cognitive load** | The demand on limited attention; when it rises, muscle tension (musculoskeletal load) rises too | 24 · [Performance](concepts/performance-cognition-mind-body.md) |
| **Cognitive reappraisal** | Reinterpreting an emotional event to lessen its impact; usually effective (unlike suppression) | 24 · [Performance](concepts/performance-cognition-mind-body.md) |
| **DARN** | Change-talk prompts: Desire, Ability, Reasons, Need | 60 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **Dichotomous thinking** | Seeing foods or behaviour as purely good or bad | 41 · [Eating & Weight](concepts/eating-emotions-weight.md) |
| **Dietary pattern** | The totality of what a person habitually eats and drinks, with the parts acting together on health | 34, 36 · [Dietary Patterns](concepts/healthy-dietary-patterns.md) |
| **Disinhibited eating** | Overeating after feeling you've "broken a rule" | 41 · [Eating & Weight](concepts/eating-emotions-weight.md) |
| **Distress** | Stress perceived as overwhelming; harms performance, health and longevity | 15 · [Movement Benefits](concepts/movement-benefits.md) |
| **Dunbar's number** | A suggested cognitive limit on the number of stable relationships a person can maintain | 30 · [Social](concepts/social-wellness.md) |
| **Embodied cognition** | The theory that the whole body, not just the brain, takes part in thinking and producing behaviour | 26 · [Performance](concepts/performance-cognition-mind-body.md) |
| **Emotional granularity** | The ability to tell emotions apart precisely, allowing measured responses | 21 · [Stress](concepts/stress-and-recovery.md) |
| **Empathy** | Understanding and sharing another person's feelings; not the same as sympathy | 49, 54 · [Coach's Role](concepts/coach-role-relationship.md) |
| **Equity awareness** | Recognising that people don't start from the same place | 62 · [Cultural Awareness](concepts/cultural-awareness-philosophy.md) |
| **Eustress** | Positive stress that drives growth and adaptation | 15, 23 · [Stress](concepts/stress-and-recovery.md) |
| **Explicit / implicit bias** | Judgements held consciously / stereotypes held without awareness | 62 · [Cultural Awareness](concepts/cultural-awareness-philosophy.md) |
| **External / internal locus of control** | Believing outcomes depend on others or chance / on your own actions | 7 · [Locus of Control](concepts/locus-of-control-attribution.md) |
| **Five Ps** | Purpose, Principles, Practices, Pillars, People: a structure for a coaching philosophy | 63 · [Philosophy](concepts/cultural-awareness-philosophy.md) |
| **Fixed / growth mindset** | Believing abilities are set / believing they grow with effort and learning | 2, 24, 60 · [Growth Mindset](concepts/growth-mindset.md) |
| **Four Fs** | Fight, flight, food and sex: hypothalamus-driven survival motivations | 25 · [Performance](concepts/performance-cognition-mind-body.md) |
| **Generative accountability** | Accountability reframed as holding clients capable and able: growthful, engaged and mutual, not nagging | 50, 54 · [Coaching Mindset](concepts/coaching-mindset.md) |
| **Get to vs have to** | The wellness mindset of choice and self-care ("I get to") rather than obligation and self-control ("I have to") | 70 · [Get To vs Have To](concepts/get-to-vs-have-to.md) |
| **Imposter syndrome** | Doubting your own skills and whether you have anything to offer | 50, 66 · [Coaching Mindset](concepts/coaching-mindset.md) |
| **Intuitive eating** | Eating by physiological hunger and fullness cues rather than situational or emotional cues; 10 principles | 43 · [Eating & Weight](concepts/eating-emotions-weight.md) |
| **Inverted U** | The arousal–performance curve: too little or too much arousal hurts performance; the middle is the sweet spot | 24 · [Performance](concepts/performance-cognition-mind-body.md) |
| **Locus of control** | How much control a person feels they have over their life and health | 7 · [Locus of Control](concepts/locus-of-control-attribution.md) |
| **Mastery experience** | Building on previous success to grow belief in your own skill | 52 · [Self-Efficacy](concepts/self-efficacy.md) |
| **Mindful eating** | Paying non-judgemental attention to hunger, fullness, taste, cravings and triggers while eating | 41, 43 · [Eating & Weight](concepts/eating-emotions-weight.md) |
| **Motivational interviewing (MI)** | A collaborative, client-centred communication style that explores ambivalence and evokes the client's own motivation | 10, 56 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **Movement milestone** | A meaningful physical accomplishment that is concrete evidence of growth | 53 · [Movement Motivation](concepts/movement-motivation.md) |
| **Negativity bias** | The tendency to focus more on negative than positive things; an evolutionary survival trait | 9, 21, 30 · [Negativity Bias](concepts/negativity-bias-self-compassion-shame.md) |
| **Neuroplasticity** | The brain's ability to adapt to new circumstances and challenges, including memory and learning | 15, 22 · [Movement Benefits](concepts/movement-benefits.md) |
| **OARS** | Open questions, Affirmations, Reflections, Summarising: the core MI tools | 10, 56 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **OPSISE** | The six dimensions of wellness: Occupational, Physical, Social, Intellectual, Spiritual, Emotional | 3 · [OPSISE](concepts/wellness-coaching-opsise.md) |
| **PERMA (+V)** | Seligman's wellbeing model (Positive emotion, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, Accomplishment), with Vitality added | 19 · [Positive Psychology](concepts/positive-psychology-gratitude.md) |
| **Positive psychology** | The scientific study of human wellbeing | 19 · [Positive Psychology](concepts/positive-psychology-gratitude.md) |
| **Pragmatic optimism** | Being honest about a hard situation, then choosing the most hopeful realistic next step | 20 · [Positive Psychology](concepts/positive-psychology-gratitude.md) |
| **Psychological and behavioral flexibility** | Recognising and adapting to situational demands while staying open and aware | 57 · [Flexibility](concepts/flexibility.md) |
| **Reflective listening** | Paraphrasing a client's words back to them to clarify and validate | 49, 56 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **Sedentary behaviour** | Time spent seated, reclining or lying down; exercise does not undo it | 14 · [Movement Benefits](concepts/movement-benefits.md) |
| **Self-compassion** | Common humanity, self-kindness and mindfulness towards your own struggles; not self-indulgence | 9, 55 · [Self-Compassion](concepts/negativity-bias-self-compassion-shame.md) |
| **Self-determination theory** | The theory, linked to MI, that people inherently seek growth; autonomy and competence fuel change | 10 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **Self-efficacy** | Confidence and intentionality about sustaining healthy habits on your own; the end goal of coaching | 52 · [Self-Efficacy](concepts/self-efficacy.md) |
| **Shame** | The painful belief that we are flawed and so unworthy of love and belonging | 54 · [Shame](concepts/negativity-bias-self-compassion-shame.md) |
| **Sleep inertia** | Grogginess from waking during deep sleep; avoided with 15-minute or 90-minute naps | 27 · [Sleep](concepts/sleep.md) |
| **Social capital** | Quality relationships and communities built on trust and belonging | 31 · [Social](concepts/social-wellness.md) |
| **Subjective vitality** | The perceived feeling of having enough energy | 15 · [Movement Benefits](concepts/movement-benefits.md) |
| **Summarising** | The "S" in OARS: pulling the whole conversation together | 10, 56 · [MI](concepts/motivational-interviewing.md) |
| **Toxic positivity** | Denying real suffering and insisting on focusing only on the good | 20 · [Positive Psychology](concepts/positive-psychology-gratitude.md) |
| **Transfer effect** | A positive change in one area spreading to others | 58, 59 · [Upward Spirals](concepts/upward-spirals.md) |
| **Upward spiral** | A positive feedback loop where one positive behaviour or emotion makes the next more likely | 58 · [Upward Spirals](concepts/upward-spirals.md) |
| **Vagal tone** | Described in the course as a measure of stress, linked to the 10th cranial nerve; it forms an upward spiral with positive emotion | 22, 58 · [Stress](concepts/stress-and-recovery.md) |
| **Vicarious experience** | Being influenced by identifying with someone else's success or cautionary tale | 52 · [Self-Efficacy](concepts/self-efficacy.md) |
| **Von Economo neurons** | Brain cells found only in highly intelligent, social animals, linked to regulating emotion in social contexts | 30 · [Social](concepts/social-wellness.md) |
| **Weight-neutral approach** | Focusing on health-promoting behaviours rather than weight loss as the outcome | 42 · [Eating & Weight](concepts/eating-emotions-weight.md) |
| **Wellness coaching (NASM)** | The active process of applying learned and developed strategies that lead to the mental, emotional and physical betterment of life | 3 · [OPSISE](concepts/wellness-coaching-opsise.md) |
| **Wellness vision** | The client's personal picture of what wellness means and is in service of; it sits at the top of the wellness plan | 69, 70 · [Wellness Plan](concepts/wellness-plan.md) |
