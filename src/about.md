# About the Source

## What this is

These are **Steve Flipse's personal study notes** on the National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM) course **Principles of Wellness Coaching**. The course and its video lessons are **NASM's copyrighted material**. This wiki is a private study aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASM.

## The source material

- **Format:** transcripts of the course's video lessons, numbered 1 to 73 (about 35,000 words).
- **Speakers:** the transcripts have no speaker labels. Some lessons are narrated lectures; others are expert interviews or Q&A sessions (for example lessons 20, 22, 25, 38, 45, 48, 51, 55, 59 and 61). Quotes in this wiki are cited by lesson number only, because the speaker is not identified.
- **Quality:** the transcripts are auto-captured. Some lessons are fragments, some sentences are cut off, and a few words are garbled. Garbled passages were left out instead of guessed at. Lesson 65 has no transcript at all.

## What is and is not reproduced

- **The transcripts are not reproduced here.** Every concept page is a summary in my own words.
- Each page includes **at most three short quotes**, each under about 25 words, so the original voice comes through on key points.
- Tables of models and acronyms (OPSISE, OARS, DARN, CAT, PERMA, the five Ps, the ten principles of intuitive eating) list what the course names. The explanations are paraphrased.
- Nothing has been added from outside the course. Where a lesson cites a statistic or study, the wiki repeats it as the course states it, without checking it against the original research.

## How the wiki is organised

- 73 lessons are grouped into **24 concept pages** in nine parts: foundations, client psychology, movement, positive psychology/stress/recovery, sleep, social wellness, nutrition, coaching skills, and wellness plans.
- Each concept page lists **which lessons it covers**, then gives key ideas, frameworks, **Coaching in practice** (example questions and phrases), short quotes, and **Related** links.
- The [Lesson Map](lesson-map.md) shows where every lesson went. The [Glossary](glossary.md) collects definitions.

## Gaps in the source

- **Lesson 65** (Coaching Philosophy, Creating 2): no transcript.
- **Very short lessons** (a paragraph or less): 13 Muscle, 16 Client-Centred Expectations, 40 Client Questions, 45 Supplementation Q&A, 47 and 48 Helping One Person, 64 Creating a Coaching Philosophy. These are folded into larger pages.
- **Lesson 53** is titled "Get to vs Have to", but its transcript is about finding meaning in movement. The "I get to / I have to" wording itself appears in lesson 70. See [Get To vs Have To](concepts/get-to-vs-have-to.md).
